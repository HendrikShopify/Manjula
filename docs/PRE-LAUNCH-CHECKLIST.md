# Pre-launch checklist

Test each item on desktop and on a phone. Tick it when it works. If it does not work, write a short note under it.

## Header
- [ ] Logo goes to the home page
- [ ] Menu links and dropdowns open the right page
- [ ] Mobile menu opens, closes and scrolls
- [ ] Header changes from see-through to solid when you scroll
- [ ] Country / currency selector changes all prices (cards, product page, add to cart button, cart)
- [ ] Account link opens the login page
- [ ] Cart (n) shows the right number and opens the cart drawer

## Home page
- [ ] Hero shows and the button works
- [ ] Product tiles link to the right product
- [ ] Image gallery and press section show
- [ ] UGC slider: swipe, Next button, videos play, the product card under the video links to the product
- [ ] Bundle offer: price and saving are correct, View details opens the quick view, the button adds the set to the cart

## Product pages
- [ ] Gallery: swipe on mobile, thumbnails on desktop, zoom, videos play
- [ ] Price, size, rating and subtitle are correct
- [ ] One-time purchase adds to cart at the normal price
- [ ] Subscribe & save: 15% off, frequency select (every 2, 3 or 4 months), adds to cart with the plan
- [ ] Ritual builder: pick a second oil, see the free mini, add all to cart
- [ ] Delivery estimate shows a correct date
- [ ] Benefits, USP tags and scent show the right content
- [ ] Community videos play in the tile, one at a time
- [ ] Judge.me reviews load, and you can write a review
- [ ] Trial results, comparison, use guide, ingredient explorer, results timeline and FAQ show and work
- [ ] Check every product: Hair Oil, Body Oil, Face Oil, Essentials Set, Discovery Set, Gua Sha, gift card
- [ ] Mini product pages send visitors to the full oil

## Cart drawer
- [ ] Drawer opens after add to cart
- [ ] Change quantity and remove items
- [ ] 1 oil: the mini offer shows, and its add button works
- [ ] 2 different oils: the free mini is added (one-time and subscription)
- [ ] Third oil or removing an oil: the free mini is removed or swapped correctly
- [ ] Subscription lines show the frequency and the 15% saving
- [ ] Cart count in the header matches the drawer
- [ ] The /cart link opens the home page with the cart drawer
- [ ] Checkout button goes to checkout

## Science page
- [ ] Concerns, ritual and botanical farm sections show
- [ ] Ingredient explorer: switching ingredients works

## About page
- [ ] Video statement plays
- [ ] Timeline and heritage sections work
- [ ] Press grid shows

## Subscription page
- [ ] Video statement plays
- [ ] Horizontal scroll works with mouse, trackpad and touch
- [ ] FAQ opens and closes

## Contact page
- [ ] FAQ categories switch and the questions open and close
- [ ] Contact form sends, and the email arrives in the right inbox
- [ ] Error messages show for empty or wrong fields

## Landing pages
- [ ] Bhringraj landing page: hero, tabs, spec table, FAQ and product picks work

## Blog
- [ ] Blog page lists the articles
- [ ] Articles: images load, share buttons work

## Footer
- [ ] Newsletter signup works and shows the success message
- [ ] New signups appear in Shopify customers
- [ ] Footer links and social links open the right page

## Other
- [ ] Cookie banner shows, and accept and decline both work
- [ ] 404 page shows for a wrong URL
- [ ] Password page (if used before launch)
- [ ] Sharing a link shows the right title and image

## Browsers and devices
- [ ] Chrome (desktop and Android)
- [ ] Safari (Mac and iPhone)
- [ ] Firefox
- [ ] No text is cut off anywhere
- [ ] No sideways scroll on mobile
